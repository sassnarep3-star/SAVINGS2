How to structure $250,000, $500,000, and $1,000,000+ savings portfolios for 100% FDIC insurance coverage and maximum yield as of October 22, 2026.

How to Maximize Both Return AND 100% FDIC Insurance Coverage (Principal + Accrued Interest)

Under federal law (12 C.F.R. Part 330), the standard FDIC insurance limit of $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category covers both principal and accrued interest. Therefore:
• If you deposit exactly $250,000 in a Single Account with no beneficiaries: Your $250,000 principal is 100% insured on Day 1 (2026-10-22), but as your ~$760/month in interest accrues and compounds, any balance above $250,000 would exceed the single-account cap unless you either (1) designate at least 1 Payable-on-Death (POD) beneficiary (which immediately raises your single-bank FDIC coverage to $250,000 per beneficiary up to $1,250,000 under 12 C.F.R. § 330.10), (2) open as a Joint Account ($500,000 cap for 2 co-owners), or (3) deposit $241,000 so the full year's ~$8,796 interest compounds inside the $250,000 ceiling.

Includes the landmark April 1, 2024 FDIC Trust Account Rule revision unifying informal POD/ITF and formal trusts up to $1,250,000 per owner per bank.

Single Accounts (Individual with no beneficiaries) 12 C.F.R. § 330.6 $250,000 per owner, per insured bank $250,000 Applies to deposit accounts owned by one person individually with no named beneficiaries (POD/ITF). All single accounts held by the same person at the same bank charter are aggregated. 2024-04-01 Verified FDIC.gov ↗
Joint Accounts (Two or more co-owners with no beneficiaries) 12 C.F.R. § 330.9 $250,000 per co-owner, per insured bank $500,000 (for 2 co-owners) Each co-owner must be a natural person, have equal withdrawal rights, and have personally signed the account signature card (or completed electronic authentication). Insured separately from each owner's Single Account. 2024-04-01 Verified FDIC.gov ↗
Trust Accounts (POD/ITF Informal Revocable, Formal Revocable & Irrevocable Trusts) 12 C.F.R. § 330.10 $250,000 per owner per unique eligible beneficiary (up to 5 beneficiaries = $1,250,000 per owner) $1,250,000 (1 owner, 5+ beneficiaries) | $2,500,000 (2 co-owners, 5+ beneficiaries) Effective April 1, 2024, FDIC unified informal POD/ITF, formal revocable, and irrevocable trusts under a single formula: # of Owners × # of Unique Eligible Beneficiaries × $250,000, capped at $1,250,000 per owner per bank charter. Simply adding Payable-on-Death (POD) beneficiaries to a savings account shifts it from Single ($250k) to Trust coverage (up to $1.25M). 2024-04-01 Verified FDIC.gov ↗
Certain Retirement Accounts (IRAs: Traditional, Roth, SEP, SIMPLE) 12 C.F.R. § 330.14 $250,000 per owner, per insured bank $250,000 All self-directed retirement deposit accounts (e.g., IRA Online Savings and IRA CDs) owned by the same person at the same FDIC-insured bank are added together and insured up to $250,000 total. 2024-04-01 Verified FDIC.gov ↗
