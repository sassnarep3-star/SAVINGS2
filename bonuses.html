4. How to judge a bonus without being fooled

  • Divide by the holding requirement. Barclays' $200 needs $25,000 held 120 days. That is 0.8% of the balance, or about 2.4% annualised over that period — worth more than the 0.15-point gap between Barclays' 3.50% and 3.65% tiers, but it is a one-off that cannot be repeated.
  • Check what the bonus does to the rate you can accept. Several "bonus" accounts pay a low base rate after the promotion ends. The SoFi boost, for example, lasts six months; the standard APY is what you live on afterwards.
  • Bonuses are taxable interest. Barclays states the $200 is reported on Form 1099-INT.
  • Never chase a bonus by moving money you may need. Early-withdrawal or minimum-balance rules can claw back more than the bonus pays.
