Key Operational & Security Rules to Know Before Funding a National High-Yield Savings Account

• Zero-Balance Auto-Closure Deadlines: Even when an account has a $0 minimum opening deposit, national banks enforce funding windows: American Express HYSA and Marcus by Goldman Sachs may close unfunded/$0-balance accounts after 60 days; BMO Alto and Citibank close $0-balance accounts after 90 days; Barclays Tiered Savings closes $0-balance accounts after 6 months (and Barclays CDs must be funded within 14 days).
• Monthly Withdrawal & Transfer Limits: While the Federal Reserve eliminated the mandatory Regulation D 6-withdrawal rule in 2020, Ally Bank enforces a 10-withdrawal limit per statement cycle (account closure upon repeated excess) and Bank of America Advantage Savings charges a $10 fee per withdrawal over 6 per cycle. By contrast, Barclays, Marcus, Synchrony, American Express, Capital One 360, and BMO Alto permit unlimited monthly ACH transfers.
• Transfer Speed & Payment Rails: Marcus by Goldman Sachs offers free same-day ACH transfers up to $100,000 when initiated before 12:00 pm ET, and American Express HYSA offers express same-day ACH transfers. Note that BMO Alto supports ACH only (no wire transfers, checks, debit cards, or Zelle®).
