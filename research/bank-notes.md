## 1. Goldman Sachs Bank USA — Marcus by Goldman Sachs (Fed rank #5)

Checked: 2026-10-22
Sources:
- Product page: https://www.marcus.com/us/en/savings/high-yield-savings
- FAQ: https://www.marcus.com/us/en/faqs
- Deposit Account Agreement (effective June 27, 2025): https://www.marcus.com/content/dam/marcus/us/en/pdfs/Marcus_Deposit_Account_Agreement.pdf

- Account: Online Savings Account (Marcus is a brand of Goldman Sachs Bank USA; agreement states "Bank" = Goldman Sachs Bank USA)
- APY: **3.50%**, "Annual percentage yield as of October 21, 2026"; APY may change at any time (variable). No tiers mentioned.
- Minimum opening deposit: none ("No minimum deposit"; agreement: "There is no minimum deposit required to open an Online Savings Account or to earn the stated APY")
- Monthly maintenance fee: none ("No fees"; FAQ: no fees for transfers to/from the account)
- Compounding: compounded daily, credited monthly (agreement III.2)
- Maximum balance: $1,000,000 per Online Savings Account; $3,000,000 per owner across all deposit accounts (FAQ + agreement II.2)
- Promotion: referral program — 3-month promotional APY increase of 1.00% on top of stated APY for referrals; example combined APY 3.75% if one referral in a year and APY unchanged. Terms: https://www.marcus.com/us/en/savings/referrals#termsandconditions . No general cash deposit bonus seen on the product page (re-check offers in bonus pass).
- Opening rules (agreement I.1): 18+, SSN or ITIN, street address in U.S. (incl. Puerto Rico, USVI, American Samoa) or U.S. military/embassy address; natural persons only; accounts may not be opened in the name of a trust/entity. Joint accounts up to 4 owners; POD beneficiaries up to 6 (beneficiaries may not be non-citizen/non-LPR individuals or trusts/entities).
- FDIC: agreement II.3 states FDIC insures deposits up to the standard maximum per depositor/per bank/per ownership category.
- App: Marcus app referenced in FAQ (manage/transfers). App feature list: UNVERIFIED (see flagged).
- Security features: UNVERIFIED pending official security page (see flagged).
- Irregularities: none.

Checked: 2026-10-22

- Disclosures/agreement page (page date 7/15/2026): https://www.capitalone.com/bank/disclosures/savings-accounts/online-performance-savings-account/

FLAG: confirm the numeric APY equals 3.20% on the live product page; disclosures page is dated 7/15/2026.

IMPORTANT merger note printed by Capital One: "any Capital One or Discover Bank deposit accounts opened on or after May 18, 2025 are immediately counted with your other Capital One deposit accounts for determining deposit insurance coverage by the FDIC."
