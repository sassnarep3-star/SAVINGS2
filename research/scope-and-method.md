## Inclusion criteria (primary comparison)

A bank is in the primary table only if ALL of these hold:

1. It is an FDIC-insured, domestically chartered bank in the Federal Reserve top 25
   by consolidated assets (June 30, 2026 release) — i.e., one of the largest banks
   in the country, not a small/local bank.
2. It offers a **consumer savings account** that the general public can open
   **online** (nationwide availability, or availability in New York — the user's state —
   with any geographic restriction recorded).
3. It provides a consumer **mobile banking app**.

Primary list under these criteria (12 banks): JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America,
Citibank (Citi), Wells Fargo, Goldman Sachs Bank USA (Marcus), U.S. Bank, Capital One,
PNC Bank, Truist Bank, TD Bank, American Express National Bank, Ally Bank.

Excluded from the top 25, with reason (each to be supported by an official source in the notes):

- Bank of New York Mellon, State Street, Northern Trust — custody/institutional banks;
  no general-public consumer savings product.
- Morgan Stanley Bank, N.A., Morgan Stanley Private Bank, N.A., UBS Bank USA —
  deposit products tied to wealth-management relationships, not a standalone
  consumer savings account openable online by the general public.
- HSBC Bank USA, N.A. — exited U.S. mass-market retail banking (to verify/document).
- Fifth Third, BMO, First Citizens, Citizens Bank, M&T Bank, KeyBank — large
  super-regional banks. Covered in a secondary table if verifiable to the same
  standard; otherwise listed here as out-of-primary-scope with Fed rank noted.

Note on Discover: Discover Bank does **not** appear as a separate charter in the
June 30, 2026 Federal Reserve ranking near its pre-acquisition size, while Capital
One, N.A. is shown at $662,157M in consolidated assets. Capital One's acquisition of
Discover must be confirmed from official Capital One/Discover/FDIC sources and its
effect on deposit insurance documented (see bank notes). Do not assert this without
an official source.

## Benchmark for "competitive"

FDIC "National Rates and Rate Caps – October 2026" (monthly rate cap information
**as of October 19, 2026**): National Deposit Rate for **Savings = 0.38%**, Money
Market = 0.63%. Per FDIC footnote, savings rates are based on the $2,500 product tier;
source data S&P Capital IQ Pro, calculations by FDIC.
URL: https://www.fdic.gov/national-rates-and-rate-caps

## Verification rules applied to every bank row

- APY, fees, minimums, bonuses, opening rules: read from the **bank's own official
  website** (account page, rate sheet, fee schedule, FAQ, deposit account agreement).
- Date checked for every value: 2026-10-22 (unless a re-check date is recorded).
- Where the bank publishes ZIP/state-specific rates, the rate recorded is the one the
  bank publishes for the user's location (New York City, NY / ZIP 10001) if accessible
  from an official page; otherwise the field is left blank and flagged.
- If a value cannot be seen on an official page, it stays blank and is listed on the
  Flagged for Review page. Search engines/aggregators are used only to locate official URLs.
- Return projections are computed from the verified APY only, assuming: account opened
  2026-10-22, single deposit, no further deposits/withdrawals, APY unchanged for the
  whole period (savings APYs are variable — projections are illustrations, not promises),
  no monthly-fee deduction unless noted, annual compounding at the stated APY.

## Account-open date used for projections

2026-10-22 (today, per project instructions to use current dates).
Projection end dates: 1 year = 2027-10-22; 3 years = 2029-10-22; 5 years = 2031-10-22.
Deposit sizes modeled: $1,000 / $10,000 / $50,000 / $100,000 / $250,000 (the last =
standard maximum FDIC coverage for a single-ownership account at one bank; see fdic-notes.md).

Where an account's APY is tiered by balance, each deposit size uses the APY of the
tier that deposit falls into, per the bank's published tiers. If tier boundaries could
not be verified from an official page, that bank's tier-dependent cells are left blank.
