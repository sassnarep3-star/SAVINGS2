The Big Four Brick-and-Mortar Yield Gap: Why Standard Relationship Tiers Cost Savers $9,000+ Per Year on $250,000

Standard savings accounts at JPMorgan Chase (0.01% APY), Bank of America (0.01% APY), Wells Fargo Way2Save (0.01% APY), and Citibank NYC Standard Savings (0.03% APY) pay between $25.00 and $75.00 per year on a $250,000 max-FDIC deposit. Even when linked to Private Client or premier checking tiers, Chase Premier/Private Client Savings pays just 0.02% APY ($50/yr) and Bank of America discontinued its Preferred Rewards savings booster when transitioning to BofA Rewards™ in May 2026.
The Sole Exception Among the Big Four: Wells Fargo Platinum Savings offers new savings customers 3.25% variable APY ($8,125/yr on $250k) when depositing ≥$25,000 within 30 days (or 3.50% APY for 12 months via in-branch banker enrollment with ≥$10,000 new money).
