Reading bonus fine print (from the verified terms): (1) bonuses are one per account/customer and typically once every 1–2 years; (2) “new money” excludes funds already held at that bank; (3) closing the account early or dropping below the threshold before payout forfeits the bonus; (4) bonuses are taxable interest; (5) an expiring promotional rate doesn’t change the underlying variable APY — check what the account pays after the promo before choosing it for the bonus alone.
