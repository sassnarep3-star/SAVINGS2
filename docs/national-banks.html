Big national brick-and-mortar banks

The largest U.S. branch-network banks. All are FDIC members (certificate numbers verified via the FDIC institutions API — see FDIC & security). Checked against each bank’s official rate pages on October 21–22, 2026.

Pattern to know: these banks publish near-zero standard savings rates and reserve meaningful rates for “relationship” configurations (linked checking, combined balances) or short promotions. If you keep savings at one of these banks without meeting those conditions, you earn 0.01%–0.05% APY.

Notes per bank

Chase (JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.)

  • Rate source: Chase’s official consumer deposit rate sheet, “RATES IN EFFECT AS OF: Friday, October 16, 2026” — Chase Savings pays 0.01% APY on every balance tier ($0–$9,999 through $50,000+); Premier Savings pays 0.01% standard / 0.02% relationship on every tier. Rate sheet PDF
  • New-customer bonus: $100 (deposit $5,000–$9,999 of new money) or $200 ($10,000+), deposited within 30 days of enrollment, maintained 90 days. Offer expires 01/27/2027; not available to existing Chase savings customers. Offer page
  • Savings accounts can’t enroll in Zelle® at Chase (eligible checking required) — per the offer page’s disclosures. Chase Mobile® app is the primary digital channel. Source

Bank of America

  • Rate source: BofA’s official NY Tri-State “Deposit Interest Rates & APYs” sheet, effective October 21, 2026: Advantage Savings standard rate 0.04% APY on every balance tier (less than $5,000 up to $10,000,000+). PDF
  • Online opening limit: $250,000 — the sheet states “New Account opening limit is $250,000 in Online Channel. For larger accounts, please contact us.”
  • Fees: $8 monthly maintenance fee; waived for the first six months for new accounts, then avoidable with a $500 minimum daily balance, an Advantage Relationship Banking account, a qualifying Preferred Rewards tier, or an owner under 25. Minimum to open: $100. Personal Schedule of Fees (effective August 7, 2026)
  • No savings-specific new-account bonus was published on the rate sheet or fee schedule reviewed. ⚠ Flagged

Wells Fargo

  • Rate source: wellsfargo.com savings rates page (fetched 10/22/2026): Way2Save 0.01% APY ($0 or more); Platinum Savings standard 0.01%; relationship 0.02% (Prime Checking) / 0.05% (Premier Checking). Rates page
  • New savings customer promo: 3.25% APY on Platinum Savings balances of $25,000+ (must deposit and maintain $25,000 within 30 days of opening; new savings customers only; variable). Wells Fargo’s official account addenda document the “Platinum Savings Rate Update, Effective October 7, 2026.” Addenda PDF
  • Below $25,000, Platinum Savings earns the standard 0.01% (or relationship) rate.

Citibank

  • New York residents: Citi’s official documentation states that in NY (and CA, CT, MD, VA, DC, NV, NJ, select FL/IL markets) the APY for Citi Savings accounts is 0.03%. Fact Sheet PDF
  • Citi Accelerate Savings (~3.00% APY) is not available to NY residents — Citi’s compare page lists the eligible states; NY is excluded (also CA, CT, DC, MD, NV, NJ, VA, IL, select FL). Eligibility list · value flagged
  • Promotional rate: 3.75% for up to 3 months on new Citi Savings accounts with $25,000+ in new-to-Citibank funds (maximum interest $10,000), for accounts opened July 14 – October 22, 2026. Citi’s official fact sheet states the Promotional Rate Feature for new Citi Savings accounts is discontinued effective October 23, 2026. Offer page · Fact Sheet

U.S. Bank

  • Rate source: usbank.com Smartly Savings rate page, effective October 21, 2026: standard 0.05% APY; relationship rates with a qualifying product by CQB: under $5,000 → 1.00%; $5,000–$24,999.99 → 2.00%; $25,000+ → 3.50%. $25 minimum opening deposit; $5 monthly fee waivable. Rates page
  • A U.S. Bank promotional page advertised “Unlock up to 4.00% APY when you bundle” Smartly Savings with an eligible product and $25,000+ CQB — listed as applying to accounts opened by October 20, 2026, in select markets. ⚠ Flagged (offer window appears closed) Offer page
  • $100 Smartly Savings bonus (with $1,000+ in new deposits, $1,000 minimum balance held until 01/31/2027) was published on U.S. Bank campus offer pages for accounts opened through October 29, 2026 — eligibility requires also qualifying for the checking bonus. ⚠ Flagged (campus-page offer) Offer page

PNC Bank

  • Standard Savings rates accurate as of 10/21/2026: 0.02% ($1–$2,499.99) / 0.03% ($2,500+), plus TotalRewards boosts (up to 0.06% at Platinum). PNC reserves the right to refuse deposits that push combined savings/money-market balances above $5,000,000. Rate table
  • High Yield Savings: 3.15% APY — but the product page displays “This Product Isn’t Available in Your Area” for in-footprint ZIP codes; PNC offers it only where it does not have branches. ⚠ Flagged for NYC residents Product page
  • Virtual Wallet checking+savings bundle bonus: $100 (Virtual Wallet, $500+ qualifying direct deposits in 60 days) or $400 (Virtual Wallet with Performance Select, $5,000+ direct deposits). Valid 09/01/2026–01/07/2027. Offer page

Truist

  • Truist One Savings: 0.01% APY on all tiers (rates accurate as of 09/28/2026); $50 minimum opening deposit; $5 monthly fee with waivers; withdrawal limit fee $5 per withdrawal over six per statement cycle (max six fees per cycle). Disclosures
  • Truist Confidence Savings (a low-fee product) requires a $25 minimum opening deposit per Truist’s comparison page. Comparison page
  • No savings bonus found on Truist’s official pages reviewed. ⚠ Flagged
