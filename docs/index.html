Key findings

  • The gap is enormous and it compounds. At $250,000 (the FDIC maximum for a single ownership category), the best online national banks pay $7,750–$8,875 in year one; the big branch banks’ standard accounts pay $25–$125. Over 5 years at constant rates, that is roughly $41,000–$48,000 vs. $125. See the math →
  • Big banks reserve their real rates for relationships. U.S. Bank pays 3.50% (with checking + $25,000 combined balances) and Wells Fargo pays 3.25% to new savings customers with $25,000+ — but their standard rates are 0.05% and 0.01%. Requirements →
  • Fees can make near-zero rates negative. Bank of America’s $8/month fee ($96/year) exceeds the 0.04% interest earned on any balance below ~$240,000; Chase’s $5/month fee exceeds its 0.01% interest at every balance. Both are avoidable via waivers. Fee waivers →
  • Your location matters (you are in New York City). Citi Accelerate Savings (Citi’s ~3.00% online account) is not available to NY residents; a NY-resident Citi customer gets Citi Savings at 0.03% standard. PNC’s 3.15% High Yield Savings is only offered outside PNC’s branch footprint. Details flagged →
  • One bank caps new online savings openings at exactly the FDIC limit: Bank of America’s NY rate sheet states “New Account opening limit is $250,000 in Online Channel.” Marcus caps Online Savings Accounts at $1,000,000 per account ($3,000,000 per owner across accounts). FDIC & limits →
  • Best first-year value at $250,000 (verified offers): SoFi (~$10,025 with 6-month 4.40% boost + $400 bonus, direct deposit required) or Capital One ($9,500 = $8,000 interest + $1,500 BONUS1500). Best ongoing value: Synchrony at 3.55%. Bonus rules →

How this research was done

  • Every rate, fee, minimum and bonus in these tables was read from an official bank page or official PDF (bank domain), or from an official government source (fdic.gov, federalreserve.gov) on October 21–22, 2026. Each row links its source.
  • Where an official page displays a value only through JavaScript (so it could not be captured in the page text), the cell is marked ⚠ flagged, the value is left out or clearly labeled as reported by independent trackers, and the item is listed on the Flagged for Review page with links.
  • FDIC insured-status of each bank was verified against the FDIC’s official institutions API (certificate numbers listed on the FDIC & security page).
  • Return projections and formulas are documented on the returns page; the calculation script is in the repository at tools/returns_calc.py.
