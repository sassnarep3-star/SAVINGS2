Notes per bank

Synchrony Bank

  • Official product page shows 3.55% APY (“Special Member Rates”), no minimum deposit, no minimum balance, no monthly fees; FDIC insurance up to $250,000 per depositor per insured bank for each ownership category; 24/7 transaction monitoring; mobile app rated 4.8 (App Store) / 4.6 (Google Play) on the page. Product page
  • Synchrony is a national online bank (cert 27314), part of Synchrony Financial. Interest compounds daily; the page’s illustrative calculator separately references a lower default rate — the advertised account APY is 3.55%.

Marcus by Goldman Sachs

  • Official page: 3.50% APY as of October 21, 2026, variable; no fees, no minimum deposit; same-day transfers of $100,000 or less. Product page · no ACH/wire transfer fees (Marcus fee statement)
  • Balance limits: maximum $1,000,000 per Online Savings Account and $3,000,000 across all deposit accounts per owner; no minimum balance required. Marcus FAQ
  • Referral promotion: refer a friend new to Marcus — both earn an extra 1.00% APY for 3 months on top of the standard APY (if one referral is made in a year and the standard APY is unchanged, the combined APY would be 3.75%). Referral page
  • Offered by Goldman Sachs Bank USA (FDIC cert 33124), Member FDIC — deposits are insured up to the maximum allowed by law. FAQ

SoFi Bank, N.A.

  • Official rates page (APYs current as of 10/20/2026): High-Yield APY 3.30% (3.27% interest rate) with Eligible Direct Deposit or $5,000+ Qualifying Deposits every 31 days; Standard APY 0.80% automatic; SoFi Plus members ($10/month subscription) earn 4.50% on up to $20,000 and 3.30% on the rest. Rates page
  • New-member promotion: up to 4.40% APY (3.30% + 1.10% boost) for up to six months on a first SoFi Checking & Savings account opened by 12/31/2027 with eligible direct deposit (or $5,000 qualifying deposits) within 60 days. Product page
  • Cash bonus: $50 or $400 with eligible direct deposits ($1,000–$4,999.99 → $50; $5,000+ → $400, measured within 25 days of your first eligible direct deposit of $1 or more). Product page
  • Above $250,000: SoFi advertises access to additional FDIC insurance up to $3M through its Insured Deposit Program (a network of participating banks) when direct deposit conditions are met. Product page — see FDIC & security for how this works
  • When you open SoFi Savings, SoFi Bank also automatically opens a SoFi Checking Account in your name (per the product page).

Capital One

  • Official account-comparison page (fetched 10/22/2026): 360 Performance Savings 3.20% APY, variable, effective as of 10/21/2026, “no fees or minimums,” FDIC-insured; 360 Checking 0.10% APY; 360 CDs up to 4.40% APY; Kids Savings 2.50% APY. capitalone.com
  • BONUS1500 offer (verified on Capital One’s official offer page): open a new 360 Performance Savings account with promo code BONUS1500; deposit $20,000+ of external funds within the 15-day Initial Funding Period; hold 90 days; bonus paid within 60 days: $20,000+ → $300 · $50,000+ → $750 · $100,000+ → $1,500. Offer page
  • Capital One is unusual among online-rate banks in also operating branches and Capital One Cafés for in-person service (per its own site).
  • Discover merger: Discover Bank (cert 5649) is now inactive at the FDIC — its deposits are part of Capital One, N.A. Anyone counting FDIC coverage should treat Discover and Capital One balances as one bank. FDIC API record

American Express

  • Official page: 3.20% APY (variable), about 6× the national rate (Amex cites the FDIC national average of 0.37% as of 09/2026); no fee to open, no minimum deposit, no monthly fees; interest compounded daily, credited monthly; 24/7 customer support; manage via the award-winning Amex App. Product page
  • No ATM card, debit card or checks are provided with the savings account (everyday spending is via Amex Rewards Checking if eligible). Accounts offered by American Express National Bank, Member FDIC.
  • No new-account savings bonus published on the official page. ⚠ Flagged

Ally Bank

  • Official page: 3.10% APY, “correct as of 10/21/26”, variable; no monthly maintenance fees or minimum balance; interest compounded daily; deposits FDIC-insured up to the maximum allowed by law; savings buckets and boosters (recurring transfers, round-ups, surprise savings). Product page
  • Limit of 10 withdrawals/transfers per statement cycle — Ally doesn’t charge a fee for exceeding it but may close the account if it happens more than occasionally. Product page
  • No new-account bonus published on the official page. ⚠ Flagged
