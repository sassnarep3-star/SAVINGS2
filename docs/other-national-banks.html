How these banks change the rankings: Barclays US’s 3.65% tier (balances $250,000–$1,000,000, accurate as of 10/07/2026) is the highest verified APY at exactly the FDIC-maximum balance — $9,125/yr on $250,000, ahead of Synchrony’s 3.55% ($8,875). CIT’s 3.75% ($5,000+) is nominally higher still, but its rate sheet is dated 07/01/2026 and may be stale, and CIT deposits share First Citizens’ FDIC charter. Both were verified by the second session with bank-page quotes (see sources).

FDIC notes for this group

  • CIT Bank is a division of First-Citizens Bank & Trust Company (cert 11063): per its own disclosure, deposits held under First-Citizens and its divisions “are not separately insured” — CIT + First Citizens balances share one $250,000 limit per depositor per ownership category. Source
  • Barclays US is Barclays Bank Delaware (cert 57203) — a separate FDIC charter from every other bank in this study, so it adds a fresh $250,000 of coverage capacity. Caps: $1,000,000 max per Tiered Savings account, max 3 accounts per customer. Source
  • HSBC Bank USA, N.A. (cert 57890), TD Bank, N.A. (cert 18409), Citizens Bank, N.A. (cert 57957) and Fifth Third Bank, N.A. (cert 6672) are each separate active insured charters — certificate records linked on the FDIC & security page.
