Why some banks pay 0.01% and others 3.75%

The high-rate accounts are all deposit-gathering machines without branch networks: CIT Bank (a division of First Citizens), Barclays Bank Delaware, Goldman Sachs Bank USA, SoFi Bank, American Express National Bank, Ally Bank. The branch-based banks price savings off their own funding needs and relationship tiers — U.S. Bank, for example, pays 0.05% standard but 3.50% if you hold $25,000 in combined balances plus a Smartly Checking account. That is not a savings rate; it is a relationship rebate, and it has conditions attached.
