5. How to read these numbers

  • Year one is almost entirely about APY. At $250,000, moving from the FDIC national average (0.38%) to the best verified rate (3.75%) is worth $8,425.00 in twelve months.
  • A bonus can beat a rate — briefly. Barclays' $200 requires keeping $25,000 on deposit for 120 days; that is roughly 0.24% of the balance for the year, on top of the 3.50% APY. It does not change the ranking, but it is real money.
  • Tier cliffs matter more than headline APY. CIT pays 0.25% below $5,000 and 3.75% above it; Barclays pays 3.65% only from $250,000. Dropping below a tier can cost more than choosing a lower-rate bank.
  • Above $250,000 the constraint changes. Return is capped by how much you can keep insured at one bank — see FDIC coverage for the multi-bank structures.
